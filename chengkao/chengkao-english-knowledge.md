### 考试形式

## 第二部分：语音知识

> 出题形式：给出 4 个单词，选出划线部分读音与其他不同的一个。

### 常考字母组合发音规律
- [ ] **ea**：
  - /iː/：tea, sea, read, meat
  - /e/：bread, head, ready
  - /eɪ/：great, break
- [ ] **oo**：
  - /uː/：food, moon, room, school
  - /ʊ/：book, look, good, foot, cook
- [ ] **ou / ow**：
  - /aʊ/：house, out, now, how, flower
  - /əʊ/：soul, know, slow, grow
  - /ʌ/：country, young, touch
- [ ] **th**：
  - /θ/（清）：think, three, thank, mouth
  - /ð/（浊）：this, that, these, those, mother
- [ ] **-ed 结尾**：
  - /t/（前为清辅音）：worked, stopped, watched
  - /d/（前为浊辅音/元音）：played, lived, opened
  - /ɪd/（前为 t/d）：wanted, needed, decided
- [ ] **-s/-es 结尾**：
  - /s/（前为清辅音）：books, maps, cats
  - /z/（前为浊辅音/元音）：bags, beds, plays
  - /ɪz/（前为 s/z/ʃ/tʃ/dʒ）：buses, boxes, watches

## 第三部分：核心语法

### 1. 名词

#### 1.1 可数名词与不可数名词
- [ ] **可数名词**有单复数；**不可数名词**无复数形式
- [ ] 常考不可数名词：*information, advice, news, furniture, equipment, money, work, progress, weather*
- [ ] 不可数名词计数用：a piece of / a bit of / an item of

例句：
- I need some **information** about the new product.（不可数，不加 s）
- She gave me a piece of **advice**.（用 a piece of 计数）

#### 1.2 名词复数变化
- [ ] 一般加 **-s**：books, desks
- [ ] 以 s, x, ch, sh 结尾加 **-es**：boxes, watches
- [ ] 以辅音字母 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 **-es**：city → cities
- [ ] 以 f/fe 结尾，多变为 **-ves**：knife → knives, leaf → leaves
- [ ] **不规则变化**：man → men, child → children, foot → feet, tooth → teeth, mouse → mice

#### 1.3 名词所有格
- [ ] 单数名词 + **'s**：Tom**'s** book
- [ ] 复数名词（以 s 结尾）+ **'**：the students**'** books
- [ ] of 所有格（无生命）：the door of the room

---

### 2. 冠词

- [ ] **a/an**：泛指，辅音音素前用 a，元音音素前用 an
- [ ] **the**：特指、独一无二、第二次提及、序数词/最高级前
- [ ] **零冠词**：泛指复数、不可数名词、专有名词、三餐、球类、语言

例句：
- I bought **a** book yesterday. **The** book is very interesting.
- He is **an** honest man.（honest 首音素为元音）
- We have lunch at noon.（三餐前不加冠词）
- **The** sun rises in the east.（独一无二）

---

### 3. 代词

#### 3.1 人称/物主/反身代词
| 主格 | 宾格 | 形容词性物主 | 名词性物主 | 反身代词 |
|------|------|-------------|-----------|---------|
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | its | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

例句：
- This is **my** book. That one is **hers**.
- He hurt **himself** while playing basketball.

#### 3.2 不定代词
- [ ] **some/any**：some 用于肯定句，any 用于否定/疑问句
- [ ] **both/either/neither**：both 两者都，either 两者任一，neither 两者都不
- [ ] **all/none**：all 三者以上都，none 三者以上都不
- [ ] **each/every**：each 强调个体（可作主语），every 强调整体

例句：
- I have **some** questions. Do you have **any**?
- **Neither** of the two answers is correct.
- **Each** of the students has a book.

#### 3.3 it 的用法
- [ ] 指代前文提到的事物
- [ ] 作形式主语：**It** is important to learn English.
- [ ] 作形式宾语：I found **it** hard to finish the task.
- [ ] 指时间/天气/距离：**It** is raining. / **It** is 5 o'clock.

---

### 4. 数词

- [ ] 基数词：one, two, three...
- [ ] 序数词：first, second, third...（前常加 the）
- [ ] **hundred/thousand/million** 前有具体数字时不加 s：
  - three **hundred** students ✓
  - **hundreds** of students ✓（表示"数百"，加 s + of）
- [ ] 分数：1/3 = one **third**，2/3 = two **thirds**（分子>1 分母加 s）

例句：
- Two **thirds** of the students passed the exam.
- **Thousands** of people attended the meeting.

---

### 5. 形容词与副词

#### 5.1 比较级与最高级
- [ ] 单音节词：+**er / est**（tall → taller → tallest）
- [ ] 长单词：more / most + 形容词（more beautiful → most beautiful）
- [ ] **不规则变化**：good/well → better → best；bad/badly → worse → worst；many/much → more → most；little → less → least
- [ ] **as + 原级 + as**：as tall as
- [ ] **比较级 + than**：taller than
- [ ] **the + 最高级 + 范围**：the tallest in the class

例句：
- He is **taller than** his brother.
- This is **the most interesting** book I have ever read.
- She runs **as fast as** me.

#### 5.2 形容词与副词的区别
- [ ] 形容词修饰**名词**，作定语/表语
- [ ] 副词修饰**动词、形容词、其他副词**

例句：
- She is a **careful** student.（形容词修饰名词）
- She does her homework **carefully**.（副词修饰动词）

---

### 6. 介词

#### 6.1 时间介词
- [ ] **in**：年、月、季节、上午/下午/晚上（in 2024, in May, in the morning）
- [ ] **on**：具体某天、星期（on Monday, on May 1st）
- [ ] **at**：具体时刻、中午/夜晚（at 8 o'clock, at noon, at night）

#### 6.2 地点介词
- [ ] **in**：在……里面（大地点）in China, in the room
- [ ] **on**：在……表面 on the table
- [ ] **at**：在某点 at the door, at school
- [ ] **to**：朝向 go to school

#### 6.3 常考固定搭配
- [ ] be interested **in**（对……感兴趣）
- [ ] be good **at**（擅长）
- [ ] be afraid **of**（害怕）
- [ ] depend **on**（依靠）
- [ ] consist **of**（由……组成）
- [ ] look forward **to**（期待）
- [ ] pay attention **to**（注意）

例句：
- I am interested **in** learning English.
- The team consists **of** five members.

### 7. 动词时态

> 考试重灾区，务必掌握 8 种主要时态。

#### 7.1 一般现在时
- [ ] 表示经常性、习惯性动作或客观真理
- [ ] 第三人称单数动词加 **-s/-es**

例句：
- She **works** in a bank.
- The earth **moves** around the sun.（客观真理）

#### 7.2 一般过去时
- [ ] 表示过去某个时间发生的动作，常搭配 yesterday, last week, in 2020

例句：
- I **visited** Beijing last summer.
- He **was** a teacher ten years ago.

#### 7.3 一般将来时
- [ ] **will + 动词原形**：表将来
- [ ] **be going to + 动词原形**：表打算/计划

例句：
- I **will call** you tomorrow.
- We **are going to hold** a meeting next week.

#### 7.4 现在进行时
- [ ] **be + doing**：表示现在正在进行的动作

例句：
- She **is reading** a book now.
- Look! They **are playing** football.

#### 7.5 过去进行时
- [ ] **was/were + doing**：过去某时刻正在做

例句：
- I **was watching** TV when he came in.

#### 7.6 现在完成时
- [ ] **have/has + 过去分词**：强调对现在的影响，或持续到现在
- [ ] 常搭配：already, yet, just, ever, never, since, for

例句：
- I **have lived** here for ten years.
- She **has just finished** her homework.

#### 7.7 过去完成时
- [ ] **had + 过去分词**：过去某时间之前已完成（"过去的过去"）

例句：
- When I arrived, the film **had already started**.

#### 7.8 过去将来时
- [ ] **would + 动词原形** / **was/were going to**：从过去看将来

例句：
- He said he **would come** the next day.

---

### 8. 被动语态

- [ ] **基本结构**：**be + 过去分词**
- [ ] 各时态被动：
  - 一般现在：is/are + done
  - 一般过去：was/were + done
  - 一般将来：will be + done
  - 现在完成：have/has been + done
  - 情态动词：can/must be + done
- [ ] 只有及物动词有被动语态
- [ ] 不及物动词无被动（happen, appear, belong 等）

例句：
- The letter **was written** by Tom.
- English **is spoken** all over the world.
- The problem **can be solved** easily.

---

### 9. 情态动词

| 情态动词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---------|------|------|
| can/could | 能、可以 | I **can** swim. |
| may/might | 可以、可能 | It **may** rain tomorrow. |
| must | 必须、一定 | You **must** obey the rules. |
| should | 应该 | You **should** see a doctor. |
| need | 需要（多用于否定/疑问） | You **needn't** worry. |
| have to | 不得不（客观） | I **have to** go now. |

- [ ] **must 的否定**：mustn't（禁止）vs needn't（不必）
- [ ] 表推测：**must be**（一定是）＞ may be＞ might be＞ can't be（不可能是）

例句：
- He **must be** tired after the long trip.（推测）
- You **needn't** do it again.（不必）
- You **mustn't** smoke here.（禁止）

---

### 10. 虚拟语气

- [ ] **与现在事实相反**：if + 过去式，would/could + 动词原形
  - If I **were** you, I **would accept** the offer.
- [ ] **与过去事实相反**：if + had done，would have + 过去分词
  - If I **had studied** harder, I **would have passed** the exam.
- [ ] **与将来事实相反**：if + were to do / should do，would + 动词原形
  - If it **were to rain**, we **would stay** at home.
- [ ] **should 型虚拟**：suggest / demand / insist / order 后接 (should) + 动词原形
  - He suggested that we **(should) leave** early.

---

### 11. 非谓语动词

> 考试高频，注意区分动词作主/宾/定语/状语时的形式。

#### 11.1 不定式（to do）
- [ ] 表示具体、将来的动作，可作主语、宾语、定语、状语

例句：
- I want **to go** home.
- He has a lot of work **to do**.
- It is important **to master** English.

#### 11.2 动名词（doing）
- [ ] 具有名词性质，可作主语、宾语
- [ ] 常跟动名词的动词：enjoy, finish, mind, practice, suggest, avoid, consider

例句：
- **Reading** is my hobby.（作主语）
- I enjoy **listening** to music.

#### 11.3 现在分词（doing）与过去分词（done）
- [ ] **现在分词**：表主动、进行
- [ ] **过去分词**：表被动、完成

例句：
- The **smiling** girl is my sister.（主动）
- The **broken** window was repaired.（被动）
- **Hearing** the news, he cried.（状语）

---

### 12. 主谓一致

- [ ] **就近原则**：there be、or、either...or、neither...nor 靠近哪个就近
  - Neither he nor I **am** wrong.
- [ ] **就远原则**：with, together with, as well as 等连接，谓语与前面主语一致
  - Tom, as well as his friends, **is** coming.
- [ ] **整体名词**作主语用单数：news, math, the United States
- [ ] **each/every/either/neither + 名词**用单数

例句：
- Each of them **has** a computer.
- The news **is** good.

---

### 13. 三大从句

#### 13.1 定语从句
- [ ] 修饰名词/代词，紧跟先行词
- [ ] **关系代词**：who（人）、whom（人，宾格）、which（物）、that（人或物）、whose（所属）
- [ ] **关系副词**：when（时间）、where（地点）、why（原因）

例句：
- The man **who** is standing there is my teacher.
- This is the house **where** I lived.
- I know the reason **why** he was late.

#### 13.2 名词性从句
- [ ] **主语从句**：What he said **is** true.
- [ ] **宾语从句**：I think **that** he is right.
- [ ] **表语从句**：The problem is **that** we have no money.
- [ ] **同位语从句**：The news **that** he won spread quickly.
- [ ] 引导词：that, whether, if, what, who, when, where, why, how

例句：
- **Whether** he will come is not decided.（主语从句）
- I don't know **when** she will arrive.（宾语从句）

#### 13.3 状语从句
- [ ] **时间**：when, while, as, before, after, until, as soon as
- [ ] **条件**：if, unless
- [ ] **原因**：because, since, as
- [ ] **结果**：so...that, such...that
- [ ] **目的**：so that, in order that
- [ ] **让步**：although, though, even if

例句：
- **If** it rains, we will stay home.（条件）
- **Although** he is tired, he keeps working.（让步）
- He was **so** tired **that** he fell asleep at once.（结果）

---

### 14. 倒装与强调

#### 14.1 倒装
- [ ] **否定词开头**：Never, Little, Hardly 等置于句首，主句部分倒装
  - **Never** have I seen such a beautiful place.
- [ ] **only + 状语开头**：部分倒装
  - **Only** then **did** I realize my mistake.
- [ ] **so/neither + 助动词 + 主语**：表示"也/也不"
  - He likes tea. **So do I**.

#### 14.2 强调句
- [ ] 结构：**It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余**
  - **It was** Tom **that** broke the window.

- [ ] **get along with** 与……相处
- [ ] **give up** 放弃
- [ ] **look after** 照顾

- [ ] **set up** 建立

- [ ] **look/see/watch/read**：
  - look 看（动作）／see 看见（结果）／watch 观看（动态）／read 读

## 第五部分：完形填空技巧

> 一篇文章挖 15 空，考逻辑、词汇、语法、搭配。

- [ ] **先通读全文**，把握大意与逻辑走向，不要一上来就填
- [ ] **看前后文线索**：转折（but, however）、因果（because, so）、并列（and, also）
- [ ] **固定搭配优先**：很多空靠搭配直接锁定
- [ ] **语法线索**：时态、单复数、主谓一致
- [ ] **代入检查**：选完读一遍，看是否通顺前后一致
- [ ] **不会的先跳过**，做完上下文再回头

## 第六部分：阅读理解技巧

> 60 分，是得分关键。共 4 篇，每篇 5 题。

### 三种题型解法
- [ ] **细节题**（最多）：回原文定位关键词，找到句子直接对应
- [ ] **主旨题**（主题/main idea）：看首尾段、每段首句
- [ ] **推断题**：根据原文合理推断，不能主观臆断

### 解题步骤
1. [ ] 先看问题，圈出关键词
2. [ ] 带着问题读文章，定位相关段落
3. [ ] 找到答案所在句，对比选项
4. [ ] 排除法：绝对化选项（always, never, all）往往是错的

### 时间分配
- [ ] 每篇约 8-10 分钟，先易后难
- [ ] 遇到难句不要纠缠，先跳过

## 第七部分：补全对话

> 5 题，考日常交际用语的问答搭配。

### 常见场景
- [ ] **问候**：How are you? — Fine, thank you.
- [ ] **感谢**：Thank you. — You're welcome. / My pleasure.
- [ ] **道歉**：I'm sorry. — That's all right. / Never mind.
- [ ] **请求**：Could you help me? — Sure. / Of course.
- [ ] **邀请**：Would you like to...? — Yes, I'd love to.
- [ ] **问路/指路**：How can I get to...? — Go straight and turn left.
- [ ] **点餐购物**：What would you like? / How much is it?
- [ ] **告别**：See you. — See you later. / Goodbye.

### 解题技巧
- [ ] 注意问答逻辑对应
- [ ] 注意礼貌用语和语气

## 第八部分：书面表达（写作）

> 25 分，字数一般 **80-100 词**，考应用文（书信、通知、日记等）。

### 评分标准
- [ ] **内容完整**（覆盖题目要点）—— 最重要
- [ ] **语言准确**（少语法错误、拼写正确）
- [ ] **结构清晰**（分段、有逻辑）
- [ ] **书写规范**（卷面整洁）

### 通用写作步骤
1. [ ] **读题**：明确文体、对象、要点
2. [ ] **列提纲**：分 3 段（开头—主体—结尾）
3. [ ] **套句型**：用熟记的模板句
4. [ ] **检查**：语法、拼写、时态、字数

### 常用句型模板

**开头句**
- I am writing to tell you that...
- I'm very glad to receive your letter.
- How is everything going recently?

**主体句**
- First, ... Second, ... Besides, ...
- What's more, ... In addition, ...
- It is important for us to... / We should...

**结尾句**
- I'm looking forward to your reply.
- Thank you for your help.
- Best wishes to you. / Wish you success.

**书信格式**
```
Dear Tom,
  I'm writing to tell you... (正文)
  First, ... Second, ...
  I'm looking forward to hearing from you.
                                          Yours,
                                          Li Ming
```

## 第九部分：商务英语常用表达

> 商务管理专业常考，写作中灵活使用能加分。

- [ ] **会议**：hold a meeting, attend a conference, discuss the project
- [ ] **合作**：cooperate with, do business with, reach an agreement
- [ ] **业务**：place an order（下订单）, make a deal（达成交易）, negotiate a contract（洽谈合同）
- [ ] **感谢/致意**：Thank you for your kind cooperation.
- [ ] **邀请**：We would like to invite you to...
- [ ] **回复**：We are pleased to confirm that...

例句：
- We are glad to **do business with** your company.
- We would like to **place an order** for 100 units.

## 第十部分：高频词汇表 + 写作模板

### 高频基础词汇（分类记忆）
- [ ] **动词**：achieve, improve, develop, provide, increase, decrease, manage, discuss, decide, suggest, offer, receive
- [ ] **名词**：reason, result, progress, problem, solution, opportunity, experience, ability, knowledge, condition
- [ ] **形容词**：important, necessary, difficult, useful, helpful, successful, various, common, popular, modern
- [ ] **连接词**：however, therefore, moreover, besides, although, because, so, and, but, or

### 万能作文模板（书信）

```
Dear ____,
  I'm writing to tell you about __________(主题).
  First, __________(要点一). Second, __________(要点二).
  Besides, __________(要点三).
  I'm looking forward to your reply.
                                          Yours,
                                          Li Ming
```

### 万能作文模板（议论文/说明文）

```
  Nowadays, ____________ is very important.
  First of all, ____________. (理由一)
  What's more, ____________. (理由二)
  In my opinion, we should ____________.
  Only in this way can we ____________.
```
