# 成人高考专升本 · 英语阅读理解 60 篇（逐题精讲）

> 适用专业：商务管理（专升本·成考）
> 每篇结构：**短文 → 5 道选择题 → 参考答案 → 逐题解析（为什么选它 / 为什么排除其他项）→ 重点词汇**
> 覆盖主题：日常生活、人物故事、科普知识、社会文化、商务经济、教育学习、健康环境等成考高频题材。

## 使用说明

- [ ] **先做题，后看解析**：每篇先自己限时做（约 8 分钟），再对照解析找原因

## Passage 1 — 日常生活（A Busy Morning）

Last Monday was a very busy morning for Mr. Brown. He got up late because his alarm clock didn't ring. He had only twenty minutes to get to the office. He washed his face quickly, put on his clothes, and ran out of the house without breakfast.

On his way to the bus stop, it began to rain. Mr. Brown had no umbrella, so his clothes got wet. When he finally reached the bus stop, he found that the bus had just left. He had to wait fifteen minutes for the next one.

When Mr. Brown arrived at his office, it was already nine thirty. His manager looked at him and said, "You are late again, Mr. Brown." Mr. Brown felt very sorry. He promised that he would buy a new alarm clock and get up early the next day.

**中文翻译：**

上周一早上对布朗先生来说是一个非常忙碌的早晨。他起床晚了，因为他的闹钟没有响。他只有二十分钟赶到办公室。他匆匆洗了脸，穿上衣服，没吃早饭就冲出了家门。

在去公交站的路上，天开始下雨。布朗先生没有伞，所以衣服被淋湿了。当他终于赶到公交站时，发现公交车刚刚开走。他只好等了十五分钟才等到下一班。

当布朗先生到达办公室时，已经九点半了。他的经理看着他说："你又迟到了，布朗先生。"布朗先生感到非常抱歉。他保证第二天会买一个新闹钟并早起。

**Questions:**

**1. Why did Mr. Brown get up late?**
A. He was ill.
B. His alarm clock didn't ring.
C. He went to bed too late.
D. He forgot to set the clock.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 原文第二句 "He got up late because his alarm clock didn't ring." 直接说明原因——闹钟没响。A 项"生病"、C 项"睡太晚"文中均未提及；D 项"忘了上闹钟"与"闹钟没响"不同，属偷换概念。故选 B。

**2. How did Mr. Brown go to the bus stop?**
A. By car.
B. By bike.
C. On foot.
D. By taxi.

**答案：C**
**解析：** 原文 "On his way to the bus stop, it began to rain." 说明他是走路去车站的（否则不会淋湿衣服、需要伞）。文中未提开车、骑车或打车，故选 C（步行）。

**3. What happened when Mr. Brown reached the bus stop?**
A. The bus had just left.
B. The bus was full.
C. It stopped raining.
D. He met his manager.

**答案：A**
**解析：** 原文 "he found that the bus had just left." 直接对应 A。B、C、D 均为干扰项，文中无依据。故选 A。

**4. What time did Mr. Brown arrive at his office?**
A. Nine o'clock.
B. Nine thirty.
C. Ten o'clock.
D. Eight thirty.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 原文 "it was already nine thirty." 明确给出九点半。故选 B。

**5. What did Mr. Brown promise to do?**
A. Buy a new alarm clock and get up early.
B. Take a taxi to work.
C. Have breakfast every day.
D. Say sorry to his manager.

**答案：A**
**解析：** 原文最后 "He promised that he would buy a new alarm clock and get up early the next day." 直接对应 A。故选 A。

**重点词汇**：alarm clock 闹钟；umbrella 雨伞；manager 经理；promise 承诺。

## Passage 2 — 人物故事（A Kind Stranger）

One cold winter evening, a young woman named Lily was walking home from work. She carried a heavy bag and felt very tired. Suddenly, she slipped on the icy road and fell down. Her bag opened, and all her things spread on the ground.

A stranger who was passing by stopped at once. He helped Lily stand up and picked up her things one by one. He also asked if she was hurt. Lily was moved by his kindness and thanked him again and again. The stranger just smiled and said, "It's nothing. Anyone would do the same."

Lily never knew the stranger's name, but she remembered him. From that day on, she decided to help others whenever she could, because a small act of kindness can warm a person's heart on a cold day.

**中文翻译：**

一个寒冷的冬夜，一位名叫莉莉的年轻女子下班后步行回家。她提着一个沉重的包，感到非常疲惫。突然，她在结冰的路上滑倒了，摔了下来。她的包打开了，里面的东西散落一地。

一位路过的陌生人立刻停了下来。他扶莉莉站起来，并一件一件地帮她捡起东西。他还问她有没有受伤。莉莉被他的善意所感动，一遍又一遍地向他道谢。陌生人只是笑了笑说："没什么，任何人都会这么做的。"

莉莉始终不知道那位陌生人的名字，但她记住了他。从那天起，她决定只要有机会就去帮助别人，因为在寒冷的日子里，一个小小的善举也能温暖一个人的心。

**Questions:**

**1. What was the weather like that evening?**
A. Hot.
B. Cold.
C. Rainy.
D. Windy.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 原文开头 "One cold winter evening" 明确说明是寒冷的冬夜。虽然提到 icy road（结冰的路），但天气描述是 cold，故选 B。D 项虽可能，但文中未直接说 windy。

**2. What happened to Lily on her way home?**
A. She lost her bag.
B. She met an old friend.
C. She slipped and fell down.
D. She missed the bus.

**答案：C**
**解析：** 原文 "she slipped on the icy road and fell down." 直接对应 C。她只是摔倒了，包打开了，并没有丢包（A 错），也未提遇友（B）或赶车（D）。故选 C。

**3. What did the stranger do for Lily?**
A. He took her to hospital.
B. He called her family.
C. He gave her some money.
D. He helped her up and picked up her things.

**答案：D**
**解析：** 原文 "He helped Lily stand up and picked up her things one by one." 直接对应 D。文中未提送医、联系家人或给钱，故选 D。

**4. How did Lily feel about the stranger?**
A. Angry.
B. Afraid.
C. Moved.
D. Proud.

**答案：C**
**解析：** 原文 "Lily was moved by his kindness" 直接说明她被感动了。故选 C（moved 感动的）。

**5. What did Lily decide to do after that day?**
A. Help others when possible.
B. Never walk home again.
C. Find the stranger's name.
D. Buy a warmer coat.

**答案：A**
**解析：** 原文最后 "she decided to help others whenever she could." 直接对应 A。故选 A。

**重点词汇**：stranger 陌生人；slip 滑倒；spread 散开；kindness 善意；moved 感动的。

## Passage 3 — 科普知识（Why Do We Sleep?）

Everyone needs sleep, but why do we sleep? Scientists have studied this question for many years. They have found that sleep is very important for our health.

During sleep, our body does a lot of work. The brain organizes the information we learned during the day, which helps us remember things better. The body repairs itself and builds up energy for the next day. Children grow faster while they are sleeping because a special hormone is produced at that time.

Most adults need about seven to nine hours of sleep every night. However, many people do not get enough sleep. They stay up late to work, study, or use their phones. If people don't sleep enough for a long time, they may feel tired, have trouble thinking clearly, and become easier to get ill.

So next time you want to stay up late, think about your health and go to bed early!

**中文翻译：**

每个人都需要睡眠，但我们为什么要睡觉呢？科学家们研究这个问题已经很多年了。他们发现，睡眠对我们的健康非常重要。

在睡眠期间，我们的身体做很多事情。大脑会整理我们白天学到的信息，这有助于我们更好地记住东西。身体会自我修复，并为第二天积蓄能量。孩子们在睡觉时长得更快，因为那时会产生一种特殊的激素。

大多数成年人每晚需要大约七到九个小时的睡眠。然而，许多人睡眠不足。他们为了工作、学习或玩手机而熬夜。如果人们长期睡眠不足，可能会感到疲惫、难以清晰思考，并且更容易生病。

所以，下次你想熬夜的时候，想想你的健康，早点上床睡觉吧！

**Questions:**

**1. What is the main idea of the passage?**
A. How to use phones.
B. Why sleep is important.
C. How children grow.
D. Why people work late.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 全文围绕"睡眠为什么重要"展开（首段提出问题，中间说明作用，最后给出建议）。A、C、D 都只是文中细节，并非主旨。故选 B（主旨题看首尾与各段主题）。

**2. According to the passage, what does the brain do during sleep?**
A. It stops working.
B. It builds up energy.
C. It organizes information we learned.
D. It produces a special hormone.

**答案：C**
**解析：** 原文 "The brain organizes the information we learned during the day" 直接对应 C。B 项"储存能量"是身体做的（The body builds up energy），D 项"产生激素"是儿童长高的原因，均张冠李戴。故选 C。

**3. Why do children grow faster while sleeping?**
A. Because they dream a lot.
B. Because a special hormone is produced at that time.
C. Because they eat more before bed.
D. Because they need less sleep.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 原文 "Children grow faster while they are sleeping because a special hormone is produced at that time." 直接对应 B。故选 B。

**4. How many hours of sleep do most adults need?**
A. Three to five.
B. Five to seven.
C. Seven to nine.
D. Nine to eleven.

**答案：C**
**解析：** 原文 "Most adults need about seven to nine hours of sleep every night." 直接对应 C。故选 C。

**5. What may happen if people don't sleep enough for a long time?**
A. They will grow taller.
B. They will become rich.
C. They will learn faster.
D. They may feel tired and get ill easily.

**答案：D**
**解析：** 原文 "they may feel tired, have trouble thinking clearly, and become easier to get ill." 对应 D。A、B、C 均与文意相反或无依据。故选 D。

**重点词汇**：organize 整理；repair 修复；hormone 激素；stay up late 熬夜。

## Passage 4 — 社会文化（The Spring Festival）

The Spring Festival is the most important festival in China. It usually comes in January or February. Before the festival, families clean their houses, buy new clothes, and prepare a lot of delicious food. People believe that cleaning the house can sweep away bad luck.

On the eve of the Spring Festival, family members get together and have a big dinner. After dinner, many families watch the Spring Festival Gala on TV. Children can get red packets from their parents and grandparents. The red packets usually contain money, which stands for good luck and wishes.

During the festival, people visit their relatives and friends. They say "Happy New Year" to each other. The Spring Festival is not only a time for celebration but also a time for families to stay together.

**中文翻译：**

春节是中国最重要的节日。它通常在一月或二月到来。节日前，家家户户打扫房屋、买新衣服，并准备许多美味的食物。人们相信，打扫房屋可以扫除厄运。

在除夕之夜，家人团聚，共进一顿丰盛的晚餐。晚饭后，许多家庭会看电视上的春节联欢晚会。孩子们可以从父母和祖父母那里得到红包。红包里通常装着钱，代表着好运和祝福。

节日期间，人们走亲访友，互相说"新年快乐"。春节不仅是庆祝的时刻，也是家人团聚的时刻。

D. To make the house warmer.

**答案：B**

## Passage 5 — 商务经济（Online Shopping）

In recent years, online shopping has become more and more popular in China. People can buy almost everything on the Internet, from clothes to food. They just need a computer or a mobile phone, and then they can shop at any time without leaving home.

Online shopping has many advantages. First, it is convenient. People don't need to go to the shops and wait in long lines. Second, the prices are often lower than in real shops, because online sellers don't have to pay high rent. Third, there are more choices, and customers can compare the prices easily.

However, online shopping also has some problems. Customers cannot see the real products before buying, so sometimes the goods are not as good as they look in the pictures. Besides, it may take a few days to receive the goods. So people should be careful when shopping online.

**中文翻译：**

近年来，网上购物在中国变得越来越流行。人们几乎可以在网上买到一切，从衣服到食物。他们只需要一台电脑或一部手机，就可以足不出户地随时购物。

网上购物有许多优点。第一，它很方便。人们不必去商店排长队等候。第二，价格往往比实体店低，因为网络卖家不必支付高昂的租金。第三，选择更多，顾客可以轻松地比较价格。

然而，网上购物也有一些问题。顾客在购买前无法看到真实的产品，所以有时商品并不像图片上看起来那么好。此外，收到商品可能需要几天时间。因此，人们网上购物时应该谨慎。

**Questions:**

**1. What do people need for online shopping?**
A. A computer or a mobile phone.
B. A car.
C. A lot of money.
D. A friend's help.

**答案：A**
**解析：** 原文 "They just need a computer or a mobile phone." 直接对应 A。

**2. Why are online prices often lower?**
A. Because the goods are bad.
B. Because online sellers don't pay high rent.
C. Because there are no sellers.
D. Because people buy less.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 原文 "the prices are often lower than in real shops, because online sellers don't have to pay high rent." 直接对应 B。

**3. What is one problem of online shopping?**
A. People can't find what they want.
B. The goods are always free.
C. Customers cannot see the real products before buying.
D. Nobody delivers the goods.

**答案：C**
**解析：** 原文 "Customers cannot see the real products before buying." 直接对应 C。A、B、D 均与文意不符。

**4. How long may it take to receive the goods?**
A. A few minutes.
B. A few days.
C. A few years.
D. Immediately.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 原文 "it may take a few days to receive the goods." 直接对应 B。

**5. What does the writer advise people to do?**

**答案：C**

**重点词汇**：online shopping 网上购物；advantage 优点；convenient 方便的；rent 租金；compare 比较。

## Passage 6 — 教育学习（Learning a Foreign Language）

Many students think that learning a foreign language is very difficult. However, if you use the right methods, it can be fun and easier than you think.

First, you should practice every day. Even if you only spend fifteen minutes a day, it helps a lot. Language is like a skill, and the more you practice, the better you become. Second, don't be afraid of making mistakes. Everyone makes mistakes when learning something new. If you never speak because you are afraid, you will never improve. Third, try to use the language in real life. You can watch movies, listen to songs, and talk with others in that language.

Remember, learning a language takes time. Be patient with yourself, and one day you will find that you can speak it well.

**中文翻译：**

许多学生认为学习一门外语非常困难。然而，如果你使用正确的方法，它会比你想象的更有趣、更容易。

第一，你应该每天练习。即使你每天只花十五分钟，也会有很大帮助。语言就像一项技能，你练得越多，就会变得越好。第二，不要害怕犯错。每个人在学习新东西时都会犯错。如果你因为害怕而从不开口说，你就永远不会进步。第三，试着在现实生活中使用这门语言。你可以用那门语言看电影、听歌曲，并与他人交谈。

记住，学习一门语言需要时间。对自己有耐心，总有一天你会发现你能把它说得很好。

**Questions:**

**1. What do many students think about learning a foreign language?**

B. It is very difficult.
C. It is boring.
D. It is useless.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 原文 "Many students think that learning a foreign language is very difficult." 直接对应 B。

**2. How long does the writer suggest practicing every day?**

B. Two hours.

**3. Why shouldn't students be afraid of making mistakes?**

B. Because everyone makes mistakes when learning something new.
C. Because teachers like mistakes.
D. Because mistakes can be a joke.

**答案：B**
**解析：** 原文 "Everyone makes mistakes when learning something new." 说明犯错很正常，所以不必害怕。故选 B。

**4. According to the passage, which is a good way to practice?**

**答案：C**
**解析：** 原文 "You can watch movies, listen to songs, and talk with others in that language." 直接对应 C。

**5. What does the writer tell us about learning a language?**

## Passage 7 — 健康环境（The Importance of Water）

Water is the most important thing for life. Without water, people can only live for a few days. Our bodies are made up of about sixty to seventy percent water, so we need to drink enough water every day.

Doctors suggest that adults should drink about eight glasses of water a day. Water helps our body work well. It carries nutrients to different parts of the body and takes away waste. It also helps control our body temperature, especially when the weather is hot or when we do sports.

Many people like to drink soft drinks or coffee instead of water. However, these drinks may contain lots of sugar, which is not good for our health. Water is the best choice. So remember to drink more water and stay healthy!

**中文翻译：**

水是生命最重要的东西。没有水，人只能活几天。我们的身体大约由百分之六十到七十的水构成，所以我们需要每天喝足够的水。

医生建议成年人每天应该喝大约八杯水。水有助于我们的身体正常运转。它把营养物质输送到身体的不同部位，并带走废物。它还有助于调节我们的体温，尤其是在天气炎热或我们运动的时候。

许多人喜欢喝软饮料或咖啡来代替水。然而，这些饮料可能含有大量的糖分，对我们的健康不利。水是最好的选择。所以，记得多喝水，保持健康！

**答案：B**

## 阅读理解题型解题技巧

### 一、细节题（占比最高）
- [ ] 找**关键词**（人名、地名、数字、时间）回原文定位
- [ ] 找到对应句子，**逐词对比选项**，注意同义替换
- [ ] 警惕"偷换概念"（如把 because 改因果、把部分改全部）

### 二、主旨题
- [ ] 看**首段、尾段、各段首句**
- [ ] 正确选项要**能概括全文**，细节性选项一般是错的
- [ ] 常见问法：main idea / mainly about / best title

### 三、推断题
- [ ] 答案**分散在文中**，需要综合归纳
- [ ] 不能凭常识主观臆断，要有原文依据
- [ ] 常见问法：infer / imply / suggest / attitude

### 四、词义猜测题
- [ ] 利用**上下文**（定义、举例、对比、因果）
- [ ] 利用**构词法**（词根词缀）
- [ ] 代入法验证

### 五、排除法技巧
- [ ] **绝对化选项**（always, never, all, only）往往是错的
- [ ] **无中生有**的选项（文中没提）要排除
- [ ] **张冠李戴**的选项（把 A 的特点说成 B）要排除
